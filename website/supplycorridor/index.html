SupplyCorridor · AI safety research direction

Can an AI agent tell a trustworthy supplier from a convincing fake?

SupplyCorridor is an early-stage concept for more transparent international sourcing. The associated research project proposes a controlled evaluation of how AI agents respond to misleading supplier information and whether safeguards actually work.

01 / The problem

International trade runs on information. So do AI agents.

Buyers often need to compare websites, supplier claims, registration documents and third-party records across borders. Changing trade rules and logistics requirements add complexity. If an autonomous agent treats unreliable information as fact, it can recommend the wrong counterparty or act before risks have been checked.

The research question extends beyond international trade: how should an agent handle evidence it cannot confidently verify?

Business context

SupplyCorridor

The broader platform concept explores sourcing relationships between buyers in the UK and EU and manufacturers in India, with supplier discovery, structured requests and more trustworthy information exchange.

Safety context

Independent evaluation

The proposed research prototype would test information integrity, decision logging, uncertainty thresholds and human review in a controlled environment using synthetic or benign public test data.

Scope matters. This is an early-stage concept and proposed safety research project. It is not a live supplier-verification service, and it does not currently offer verified suppliers, buyer protection, or automated purchasing.
02 / Research design

Test the weak points. Measure the difference.

01 / Falsified identityCan agents detect fabricated supplier claims?

Use synthetic supplier pages with inconsistent registrations or invented credentials.

02 / Conflicting evidenceWill agents question sources that disagree?

Present competing records about business details, location or compliance.

03 / Misleading instructionsCan untrusted content redirect the agent?

Test whether page text causes an agent to disregard checks or hide uncertainty.

04 / Premature actionWill agents recommend too soon?

Measure decisions made before adequate evidence or human approval is available.

Incorrect acceptance rateHow often an unreliable profile is treated as credible.
Escalation qualityWhether uncertain cases are referred for human review.
Evidence traceabilityWhether decisions retain useful citations and audit records.
The research will compare baseline agent behaviour against layered safeguards, including cross-source checks, uncertainty reporting, decision logs and human review. Formal testing has not yet begun.
