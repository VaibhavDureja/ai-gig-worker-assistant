Research approach

Ask better questions. Test the answer.

AI systems can make persuasive recommendations without reliable evidence. I'm particularly interested in how organisations might test their safeguards rather than merely describing them.

01
Start with a real-world problem

Use supplier verification as a concrete example of decisions made from third-party information.

02
Define measurable failure modes

Assess misleading sources, conflicting claims, premature conclusions and failures to escalate uncertainty.

03
Share what the evidence supports

Document limitations, publish findings where appropriate and avoid claiming the research proves more than it does.
